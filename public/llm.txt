## Academic Faculties & Offerings
- **Faculty of Information Technology (FOIT)**: BS Computer Science, BS Software Engineering, BS Artificial Intelligence, BS Data Science, BS Cyber Security, ADP Computer Science.
- **Faculty of Management Studies (FMS)**: Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA Hons), BS Accounting & Finance, BS Business Analytics, ADP Business Administration, ADP Commerce.
- **Faculty of Science & Technology (FOST)**: BS Biotechnology, BS Biochemistry, BS Chemistry, BS Zoology, BS Mathematics, BS Physics.
- **Faculty of Humanities & Social Sciences (FHSS)**: BS English Language & Literature, BS Psychology, BS Clinical Psychology.
- **Faculty of Media & Mass Communication (FMMC)**: BS Media & Communication Studies, Strategic CNN Academy collaboration.

## Admissions & Scholarships
- **Admissions Open**: Fall & Spring Admissions Cycles
- **Eligibility**: Minimum 50% marks in intermediate (HSSC/FSc/FA/ICS/I.Com) or A-Levels equivalence
- **Merit Scholarships**: Up to 100% tuition waiver based on intermediate marks
- **PGC Alumni Concession**: Special financial concession for students from Punjab Group of Colleges
- **Kinship Concession**: Financial waiver for siblings of currently enrolled UCP students
- **Sports & Talent Concessions**: National & provincial athlete full scholarships
